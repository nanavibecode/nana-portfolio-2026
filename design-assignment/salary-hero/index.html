01 — Overview
Brief
As a Product Designer, my goal is to design the end-to-end loan experience within the Salary Hero app introducing a brand new product to users who already trust the platform for salary withdrawals.
This is a greenfield problem. There is no existing loan flow to reference — defining the UX logic, UI patterns, and business rationale from scratch.
Challenge
  • How might we make applying for a loan feel safe, simple, and suited to someone already using Salary Hero and for someone who has never used Salary Hero?
  • How do we build trust in a new financial product without overwhelming users or creating drop-off?
Context & assumptions
Salary Hero currently helps users access earned wages early. A loan product is a natural adjacency but it comes with different user psychology, risk considerations, and regulatory implications.
  • Loans would be offered to existing Salary Hero users and new Salary Hero users
  • Repayment may be structured through salary deductions
  • Loan amounts and eligibility may be informed by a user’s withdrawal history on the platform
Task — a loan experience that balances
  • User trust & clarity — users understand what they’re signing up for
  • Conversion — eligible users successfully complete an application
  • Business viability — the flow supports responsible lending and reduces default risk
Scope
Design work plan — research plan, delivery plan, validation plan
Deliverables
  • Problem framing & opportunity
  • Business & UX rationale
  • Success metrics (KPIs)
  • User flow & experience
  • UX UI considerations
  • Validation & next steps

03 — Users & insights

Who borrows, and what worries them

Persona A · Existing Salary Hero user

Blue-collar worker who already uses salary advance

  • Withdraws earned wages near month-end
  • Knows "it comes back on payday"
  • Needs a bigger lump sum than an advance covers
  • Can't get a bank loan — turns to loan sharks
Persona B · New to Salary Hero

Blue-collar worker discovering the app for the loan

  • Employer just offered Salary Hero
  • Unfamiliar with payroll deduction
  • Unsure if eligible or trustworthy
  • Used to informal lenders

Persona A, in their own words

Existing Salary Hero users — blue-collar workers who turned to loan sharks before.

Source: Salary Hero YouTube channel · Persona B remains a hypothesis to validate.

Shared insights — both personas

Insight 1

Sometimes need money fast — and turn to loan sharks

→ Offer a safer, cheaper option right where they already get paid.

Insight 2

Not so tech-savvy

→ An easy-to-understand app flow: few steps, plain language, one action per screen.

Insight 3

Not so financially literate

→ Introduce loans ethically, and always make clear how much and when they repay.

04 — Competitors & app inspect

How others lend, and what Salary Hero already has

Bank apps

Pao Tang · K PLUS Xpress Loan · Krungsri iFIN · ttb Cash To Go · UOB Xpress

+ Legal, regulated; up to 5× monthly income, 60–72 months

− Min. salary ฿15,000–20,000 — many blue-collar workers can't qualify

Loan apps

TrueMoney · Shopee · Dolfin · Wealthi · FINNIX · Promise

+ Fast, in-app; Promise shows balance, due date and amount clearly

− 8.99–28% / year, some 2–2.75% / month; manual repayment

Wage / payroll apps

Tanjai · Finn · Setlary · Mula-X · Noburo

+ Via partner employers, repaid by salary deduction (e.g. Noburo PAYDAY)

+ Noburo pairs loans (15% / year) with financial-habit missions

− Slow, document-heavy application (ID, bank book, credit report, interview)

Loan entry points on bank apps

Takeaway: loans are a generic icon in a menu — no eligibility, no amount, no context. Salary Hero can surface it on the home card, personalised.

Loan entry points on loan apps

Takeaway: loan apps meet users where they already chat (LINE) and make applying the first action. Salary Hero can do the same — entry via LINE OA and push, not only in-app.

Loan application on loan apps

ThaiNance — online loan app, App Store screens.

Key takeaways
  • Fast — auto-approval in 3–5 minutes
  • Easy start — phone + OTP, no documents
  • Transparent — limit, APR and term on the home card
  • Safety cue — "pay only in the app" warning
  • Costly — APR up to 35.8%, 91–365 days
What Salary Hero should take
  • Keep — limit on the first screen, apply in 3 visible steps
  • Keep — safety & scam cues, human help on screen
  • Beat — lower cost and longer terms, thanks to payroll deduction

Loan journey on wage / payroll apps

Noburo “Mission Loan” — 100+ partner companies, 100,000+ employees.

Key takeaways
  • Friendly, happy visual design — mascots and bright colours make the app feel good, not scary
  • Ethical by design — debt plan, income & spending log before lending
  • Employer-linked — company code from HR
  • Heavy — ID, selfie, bank book, credit report PDF, phone interview
  • Slow — money only after a month of missions; not for emergencies
  • Clear after borrowing — loan card with due date, amount, Pay now
What Salary Hero should take
  • Keep — a warm, friendly visual tone so borrowing feels good, not stressful
  • Keep — ethical nudges, but light and inline (take-home pay, "borrow what you need")
  • Keep — a clear loan card on home
  • Avoid — document uploads; pre-qualify from payroll & withdrawal history
  • Avoid — interviews & credit-report PDFs; too hard for low-tech users
  • Avoid — long waits; decide within 1 working day

Salary Hero app inspect

Reuse

  • Bank account already on file
  • Users know "it comes back on payday"
Watch out
  • Advance + loan hit the same payslip — show together
  • Don't crowd the withdraw action
  • No partner employer = no access — support confirms new users can't join alone
  • Support replies in hours, not minutes — too slow for an emergency loan

Salary Hero’s unfair advantage

  • Pre-qualified — income already verified
  • Can't miss a payment — deducted at source
  • Whole payslip in one view — advance + loan + take-home

05 — Assumptions

What I assumed, to design without waiting

  • Eligibility — 6+ months at a partner employer, and total deductions stay under a set share of salary
  • Limit — based on salary and withdrawal history on Salary Hero
  • Terms — fixed 3–12 months, same amount every payday
  • Decision — within 1 working day, no documents
  • Privacy — employer sees the deduction amount only
  • Leaving the job — remaining balance deducted from final salary

To confirm with Salary Hero's product, risk and legal teams.

07 — Business & UX rationale

Good for business only if good for the borrower

Business
  • New revenue, bigger ticket than advances
  • Lower risk — repaid from payroll
  • Retention — engaged every payday
  • Employer value — a wellbeing benefit
UX
  • Trust — no surprise deductions
  • Certainty over speed
  • Responsible — borrow what you need
  • No dead ends for any state

Design principles

1 · Show take-home pay, not just the installment

2 · Baht before percent

3 · Know before you apply

4 · Always a way out — early payoff from day one

10 — Prototype

Screen by screen

Illustrative: ฿20,000 · 6 months · ฿3,500/month · payday 25th.

A

Finding out

  • One card per state — no promo for those who can't use it.
  • Withdraw stays first — loan is secondary.
  • "Soon", not "no" — a date for new joiners.
  • Borrowers see status, not offers.
B

Considering

  • Take-home pay is the hero number.
  • Baht first, rate secondary.
  • Privacy answered before it's asked.
  • Honest cross-sell — small needs → salary advance.
C

Registering

  • Pre-filled — confirm, don't type.
  • Consent in real numbers — amount and end date.
  • Full schedule before signing.
  • "Nothing deducted unless approved" at submit.
D

Waiting and decision

  • A time promise, not a spinner.
  • Approval leads with repayment date.
  • Rejection — reason, smaller amount, alternative.
  • Employer isn't told.
E

Repaying and paying off

  • "Left to repay" is the hero.
  • Payslip preview before payday.
  • Pay off early is a primary button.
  • Confirms deductions cancelled.

01 — Overview
Brief
As a Product Designer, my goal is to design the end-to-end loan experience within the Salary Hero app introducing a brand new product to users who already trust the platform for salary withdrawals.
This is a greenfield problem. There is no existing loan flow to reference — defining the UX logic, UI patterns, and business rationale from scratch.
Challenge
  • How might we make applying for a loan feel safe, simple, and suited to someone already using Salary Hero and for someone who has never used Salary Hero?
  • How do we build trust in a new financial product without overwhelming users or creating drop-off?
Context & assumptions
Salary Hero currently helps users access earned wages early. A loan product is a natural adjacency but it comes with different user psychology, risk considerations, and regulatory implications.
  • Loans would be offered to existing Salary Hero users and new Salary Hero users
  • Repayment may be structured through salary deductions
  • Loan amounts and eligibility may be informed by a user’s withdrawal history on the platform
Task — a loan experience that balances
  • User trust & clarity — users understand what they’re signing up for
  • Conversion — eligible users successfully complete an application
  • Business viability — the flow supports responsible lending and reduces default risk
Scope
Design work plan — research plan, delivery plan, validation plan
Deliverables
  • Problem framing & opportunity
  • Business & UX rationale
  • Success metrics (KPIs)
  • User flow & experience
  • UX UI considerations
  • Validation & next steps

Key insights — both personas

Blue-collar employee, money in need

Insight — in their wordsUX opportunity
1 · Need cash, but have no credit history“What if I get rejected?”Check eligibility first, and show how much they can borrow upfront. If rejected, explain why — softly.
2 · Fixed costs, little left over“How much salary will I have left?”Show take-home pay and let them choose how much salary to keep each month. Borrowing too much triggers a red warning.
3 · Money can't wait (ร้อนเงิน)“Banks are slow.”If they're eligible, make applying as frictionless as possible — pre-filled, no documents.
4 · Loan terms feel confusing“Interest is confusing.”Lead with the total cost in baht; show the rate second.
5 · Repayment is invisible“How much is left to pay?”Keep a loan card visible — balance, next deduction, end date.
04 — Competitors & the Salary Hero app

How others lend, and what Salary Hero already has

Bank apps

Pao Tang · K PLUS Xpress Loan · Krungsri iFIN · ttb Cash To Go · UOB Xpress

+ Legal, regulated; up to 5× monthly income, 60–72 months

− Min. salary ฿15,000–20,000 — many blue-collar workers can't qualify

Loan apps

TrueMoney · Shopee · Dolfin · Wealthi · FINNIX · Promise

+ Fast, in-app; Promise shows balance, due date and amount clearly

− 8.99–28% / year, some 2–2.75% / month; manual repayment

Wage / payroll apps

Tanjai · Finn · Setlary · Mula-X · Noburo

+ Via partner employers, repaid by salary deduction (e.g. Noburo PAYDAY)

+ Noburo pairs loans (15% / year) with financial-habit missions

− Slow, document-heavy application (ID, bank book, credit report, interview)

Loan entry points on bank apps

Loan entry points on loan apps

07 — Business & UX rationale

Why a loan fits Salary Hero — and how the UX keeps it safe

Why it fits

Salary Hero already has verified income and repayment through payroll — so it can lend with lower risk and lower cost than banks or loan apps, to users who already trust it.

Value for both

User — a safer, cheaper option than loan sharks.
Business — new revenue, lower default risk, users engaged every payday.

Stage 1

Find out

How the Salary Hero home changes for each user — eligible users see their limit upfront; everyone else sees no offer, just a way to learn.

AddressesCheck credit historyExisting userNot a user yet
  • Built on today’s home — the loan joins Salary on Demand, never pushes Withdraw down (A1 → A2).
  • Limit upfront — a loan card, a Loan shortcut and a Special Offer, only for eligible users (A2, A4).
  • No dead end — not eligible means no offer, how it works and how to become eligible (A3, A5 → A7).
  • Where they already are — existing users meet the loan inside Salary on Demand (A6); new users get trust cues (A4).
  • Privacy in plain words — “Your manager and team won’t know” eases the fear of being judged at work, without over-promising: payroll still processes the deduction (A4, B1).
Stage 2

Decide

One place to answer: what does it cost, and what’s left of my salary.

AddressesLittle left overConfusing loan terms

  • Baht before percent — monthly amount and total cost first (B1).
  • The hard questions first — “what if I leave my job?” (B3).
Stage 3

Apply

Four short steps, no paperwork, no waiting — eligible users are pre-approved.

AddressesMoney can't waitConfusing loan terms
  • Pre-filled — confirm, don’t type (C1).
  • Consent in real numbers — amount and end date, full schedule (C2).
  • e-KYC once — ID card + face scan, only on the first loan; skipped if already verified (C3).
  • No approval wait — money sent right after signing (C4–C5).
  • First deduction date on the success screen (C5).
Stage 4

Repay

Always visible, always adjustable — and easy to leave.

AddressesInvisible repaymentLittle left over

  • Payslip preview before payday — no surprise deductions (D3).
  • Adjust the deduction — lower or higher, new end date shown (D4).
  • Pay off early — saving shown; future deductions cancelled (D5–D6).
